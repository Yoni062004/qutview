The problem

The UAE measures food security by price. Price isn't the risk.

The UAE imports 85–90% of its food, and its strategy aims to rank #1 in the world for food security by 2051. Yet resilience is tracked by price and reserves — not by corridor risk: how dangerously concentrated each import corridor is, and which supplier a commodity can't survive losing.

The solution

It scores the structure of every corridor — and warns early.

Built on public, authoritative data — UN Comtrade, the World Bank, FAOSTAT. For each corridor, automatically:

Why it matters

Insights nobody else surfaces — every figure from real data.

The proof that corridor risk is a real, unwatched dimension:

Big ≠ fragile

Dairy is the largest food import (~$1B/yr) yet low-risk — well diversified across ~60 origins. QUTVIEW tells big from fragile, so its red flags are trusted.

The feed-dependency lens

The UAE's "local" poultry, dairy and meat secretly run on three imported feed corridors. No other dashboard shows this cross-silo exposure.

It caught its own false story

An apparent supplier "surge" was reporting lag — the value was flat; unreported origins shrank the base. The tool corrected a narrative we believed.

The product

A live system — built to be trusted.

One engine, three surfaces (an interactive dashboard, a Power BI report, and grounded AI briefs), engineered so it cannot overclaim: every figure carries its provenance, every reconstructed year is labelled, every caveat is disclosed. For a sovereign risk tool, that credibility is the product.
